About Arunachalam Transport

Trusted bulk transportation experience since 2011.

Arunachalam Transport Company has been serving the bulk material transportation sector since 2011, with a focus on moving fly ash from thermal power plants to cement factories and transporting cement to customer destinations.

We understand the importance of dependable vehicle coordination, planned dispatches and timely delivery in industrial supply chains. Our operations are focused on supporting thermal power plants, cement manufacturers and associated industries through dedicated tanker transportation.

Our Core Operations

Thermal Power Plants to Cement Factories — Bulk Material Transportation

We specialize in the transportation and movement of bulk fly ash from thermal power plants to cement factories, along with bulk cement transportation and shipping support.

◈

Fly Ash Transportation

Moving bulk fly ash from thermal power plants to cement factories is a key part of our operations. We coordinate tanker transportation to support the movement of material between source plants and receiving factories.

  • Thermal power plant to cement factory transportation
  • Bulk fly ash movement using tanker vehicles
  • Dispatch, route and delivery coordination
  • Support aligned with customer movement schedules
▰

Cement Transportation & Shipping

We support the transportation of bulk cement from cement manufacturing and supply locations to customer destinations, helping keep industrial and distribution supply chains moving.

  • Bulk cement transportation by tanker
  • Movement from cement factories to required destinations
  • Transport coordination for cement manufacturers and customers
  • Route and delivery planning based on requirements

Our focus is dedicated bulk transportation for the cement and fly ash industries. Contact us to discuss routes, capacity and dispatch requirements.
