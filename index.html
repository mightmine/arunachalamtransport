Our Services

Bulk Material Transportation

Arunachalam Transport Company focuses on the transportation of two essential bulk materials — cement and fly ash. We support customers with dedicated tanker transportation and dependable movement coordination.

▰

Cement Transportation

We provide road transportation for bulk cement using tanker vehicles, supporting the movement of cement from supply points to customer destinations.

  • Bulk cement movement by tanker
  • Transport support for cement plants, distributors and construction-related customers
  • Route and delivery coordination based on customer requirements
◈

Fly Ash Transportation

We provide tanker transportation for bulk fly ash, helping industrial and infrastructure customers move this material from collection or supply locations to their required destinations.

  • Bulk fly ash movement by tanker
  • Transport support for power, cement and construction-related industries
  • Delivery planning and coordination according to customer schedules

For availability, routes, load requirements and delivery schedules, please contact our team.
